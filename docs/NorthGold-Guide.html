How to read this

Sections 1 to 5 explain what the platform does — the rules it follows with a member's money. Sections 6 to 8 are practical: what each screen is for and what you can adjust. Sections 9 and 10 cover the safeguards built in, and the handful of things to set before opening to the public.

One idea underpins everything in this document A member is promised an exact schedule of payments at the moment they invest. Every rule here exists so that promise is kept precisely — not approximately, not generously, and never changed after the fact.

1 · What NorthGold is

NorthGold takes a member's deposit, places it in a fixed-income plan, and pays them an agreed percentage back every month for a full year. Members who introduce other members earn a share of what those people deposit and of what they are later paid.

Three things it does

It pays monthly, not at the end

A member sees the entire twelve-month payment schedule before they commit a rupee. Money reaches their wallet every month, and they can withdraw it or leave it. There is nothing to watch, nothing to time, and no surprises at maturity.

Every rupee is verified by a person

Deposits and withdrawals are cash, handed over at your counter. Nothing moves in the system until one of your staff has checked it against the receipt and approved it. There is exactly one way money comes in, one way it goes out, and one person accountable for each.

It rewards people who bring people

Every member has a referral link. Anyone joining through it is placed beneath them, and earnings flow upward through five levels — so a member benefits not only from who they introduce, but from who those people introduce in turn.

What a member gets

  • A personal dashboard showing balance, active investments and network
  • The full payment schedule for every investment they hold
  • A wallet with every movement listed and explained
  • A referral link, a live view of their network, and a record of every rupee it has earned them
  • A support button that opens a WhatsApp conversation with your desk

What you get

  • A queue of deposits and withdrawals waiting for approval
  • Platform-wide totals: money in, money out, obligations outstanding
  • Full control of the plans, the rates and the referral structure
  • A searchable member list with the network beneath each person

2 · How money moves — the full cycle

This is the entire product in seven steps. Everything else in this document is detail on one of them.

  1. The member hands over cash at your counter and takes a receipt.
  2. They file a deposit request on the website: the amount, the receipt number, and a written description of the handover — who took it, where, and when.
  3. Your staff verify and approve it. Until this happens, nothing in the system has changed. No balance, no investment, no earnings.
  4. The wallet is credited and an investment opens on whichever plan covers that amount. The member can now see their exact twelve-month schedule.
  5. Referral earnings are paid to the people above them — their introducer, that person's introducer, and so on up to five levels.
  6. Every month, the scheduled amount is paid into the member's wallet. Referral earnings are paid again on each of those payments.
  7. At the end of the year the original deposit is returned to their wallet and the investment closes.

And when they want their money

The member requests a withdrawal and says when they will collect. The amount is set aside immediately so it cannot be spent twice. Your staff confirm the hand-over, or decline with a reason — in which case the money goes straight back to the member's available balance.

Nothing happens automatically that involves your cash Money only enters the system when a member of your staff approves it, and only leaves when a member of your staff confirms the hand-over. The platform never moves cash on its own; it only tracks and calculates.

There are four plans. The deposit amount decides which one applies — the member does not choose, and cannot be placed in the wrong tier.

Two things make members stay

A bigger deposit earns a higher rate. Move from $4,999 to $5,000 and the yearly return goes from 19.75% to 25%.

A longer hold earns a higher rate. Within every plan the monthly percentage rises as the year goes on. Month twelve always pays more than month one, so withdrawing early means giving up the best months.

Points members ask about

  • The percentage applies to the original deposit, not to a growing balance. Returns do not compound unless you switch that on.
  • Money is paid to the wallet, where it can be withdrawn or reinvested at any time.
  • The original deposit comes back in full at the end of the year, on top of everything already paid.
  • The schedule cannot change. See section 9.

4 · Deposits and withdrawals

Taking a deposit

  1. The member brings cash to your counter and receives a receipt.
  2. On the website they enter the amount, the receipt number, and a description of the handover. The description is required — cash leaves no bank trail, so this is the only thing your staff can check the money against.
  3. The request appears in your queue marked pending. No balance has changed.
  4. Your staff compare it with the counter receipt and either approve it or decline it with a reason, which the member sees on their own screen.

Paying a withdrawal

  1. The member requests an amount and says when they intend to collect.
  2. The amount leaves their available balance immediately. It is set aside, so the same money cannot be promised to two requests.
  3. Your staff arrange the collection and confirm the hand-over in the panel.
  4. If declined, the money returns to the member's available balance at once, with the reason shown.

The three balances a member sees

BalanceWhat it means
AvailableCan be withdrawn or invested right now.
Locked in investmentsDeposits currently earning monthly returns. Returns at the end of the year.
PendingA deposit awaiting your approval, or a withdrawal already set aside awaiting collection.

Every movement is recorded

The member's wallet lists every change to their balance with a plain explanation and a date: money coming in, money placed into an investment, each monthly payment, each referral earning, money set aside for a withdrawal, and money returned if one is declined. Nothing changes silently and nothing can be edited afterwards.

Approval cannot be undone from the panel Approving a deposit credits the member, opens an investment and pays referral earnings to several other people in the same instant. There is no undo button, because reversing it would mean taking money back from people who have already been paid. Verify before approving.

5 · Referral earnings

Every member has a referral link. Anyone who joins through it sits beneath them, and earnings travel upward through five levels.

The five levels

LevelWho they are Share of their deposit Share of their monthly payment Unlocked after
1Someone you introduced yourself5.0%10.0%—
2Someone they introduced3.0%5.0%1 referral
3One level deeper again2.0%3.0%2 referrals
41.0%2.0%3 referrals
50.5%1.0%4 referrals

"Unlocked after" is how many people a member must have personally introduced before that level starts paying them — it rewards members who keep building rather than resting on one good introduction.

Earnings are paid twice

Once when the person below deposits, and again on every monthly payment they receive for the rest of the year. The second is far larger over a full term, and it is what makes an active network worth more than a one-off sign-up.

Worked example

Priya introduced Rahul, who introduced Anita. Anita deposits $900 (Starter plan), then receives roughly $9–$13 every month.

Rahul — 5% of her deposit, once$45.00
Priya — 3% of her deposit, once$27.00
Rahul — 10% of each monthly payment$0.90 – $1.35

When a level pays nothing

If a member has not yet unlocked a level, that payment is skipped — and the skip is recorded with its reason in their own earnings history. They can see exactly why a payment did not arrive, which turns a support complaint into an answer they can read for themselves.

6 · Using the member website

Every screen a member can reach, and what it is for.

  • Home Explains the plans, shows each one's month-by-month curve, and includes a calculator that works out the exact schedule for any amount — using the same calculation that will later pay it.
  • Open an account Name, email and password. A referral code is optional; if someone enters one that does not exist it is ignored rather than blocking the sign-up.
  • Dashboard Balance, money invested, lifetime returns and referral earnings — then the next payments due, active investments, and the network as a diagram.
  • Wallet Make a deposit, request a withdrawal, and read every movement on the account, each with a date and an explanation.
  • Investments Every investment held, how far through the year it is, what it has returned so far, and its full month-by-month schedule.
  • My Network The member's referral link to share, the tree of people beneath them, what each of those people has earned them, a breakdown by level, and a full earnings history including skipped payments and why.
  • Profile Personal details, password, and identity document upload for verification.
  • Support A button on every signed-in page. The member picks a common question or writes their own, and it opens a WhatsApp conversation with your desk, message ready to send.
  • About and Contact Public pages. Contact shows your phone, WhatsApp, email, counter address and hours — all taken from your settings, so one change updates the website.

A first-time member's path

  1. Read the plans on the home page and try the calculator.
  2. Open an account.
  3. Bring cash to the counter and take a receipt.
  4. File the deposit on the Wallet screen with the receipt number and a description of the handover.
  5. Once approved, the investment appears with its full schedule — and the referral link on My Network is ready to share.

7 · Running the administrator panel

The panel does two jobs: verifying money movements, and setting the rules that govern them. Only staff accounts can open it.

The daily routine

  1. Open the Dashboard. It leads with the queue: how many deposits and withdrawals are waiting, and for how much.
  2. Go to Deposits. Each request shows the member, the amount, the receipt number and their description of the handover.
  3. Check it against your counter receipt and approve — or decline, giving a reason the member will read.
  4. Go to Withdrawals. The money is already set aside. Arrange the collection, then confirm the hand-over. Declining returns the money to the member.

Members and verification

The Users screen lists every member with their balances and referral code, searchable by name, email or code. Selecting one draws the network beneath them. Identity documents awaiting review appear at the top of the same screen for approval or rejection.

8 · What you can change yourself

All of the following are editable in the panel, with no developer involved.

Investment plans

Change the deposit range for each plan, the length of the term, and the percentage paid in every individual month. You can make a plan more generous, less generous, longer or shorter.

Editing a plan never affects anyone already invested Each investment keeps its own copy of the schedule, taken at the moment the member invested. Your edits apply only to investments opened from then on. Nobody's promised return can be changed after the fact — not by accident, and not deliberately.

Referral structure

Change what each of the five levels pays on a deposit and on a monthly payment, and how many personal referrals are needed to unlock each level. You can also limit how many levels deep earnings travel.

9 · Rules that protect you and your members

These are built in and cannot be switched off. Each one exists because the obvious alternative causes a real problem.

A promised schedule can never be changed

When a member invests, the platform takes a permanent copy of that plan's payment schedule. Later edits to the plan cannot touch it.

Why it matters — a member read a specific schedule before handing over cash. Without this, adjusting a rate would silently rewrite what every existing member had been promised, and you would have no record of what you originally agreed.

Money is always rounded down

Every payment is rounded down to the cent, never up.

Why it matters — rounding up gains a fraction on roughly half of all payments. Across twelve months and five referral levels, that quietly pays out more than the schedule promised.

A month can never be paid twice

Each investment records how many months it has been paid. The system only ever pays the next unpaid month, and only once its date has arrived.

Withdrawal money is set aside immediately

The moment a member requests a withdrawal, the amount leaves their available balance.

Why it matters — otherwise the same balance could back two requests at once, and you would approve the second against money that no longer exists.

Nothing is deleted or edited

Every movement is written down permanently with the amount, the resulting balance and an explanation. Balances are never quietly adjusted. If a figure changed, there is a line explaining why.

Payment dates never drift

A deposit made on the 31st is paid on the 28th in February, not carried into March. Payment dates stay inside the month they belong to.

10 · Before you go live

Set these first

  1. Your WhatsApp number. The support button and the contact form both use it, and it ships with a placeholder. Until you change it, those buttons open a number that does not exist.
  2. Your phone, email, counter address and opening hours. These appear on the public contact page.
  3. Review the four plans. The rates supplied are examples. Confirm every percentage is one you are willing and able to pay for a full year before a single member sees them.
  4. Review the referral levels. Referral earnings are paid on every monthly payment, so their cost accumulates across the whole year, not just at sign-up. Model that total before opening.
  5. Create staff accounts for whoever will approve deposits, and no one else.

Work out your obligation before you promise it

Every approved deposit commits you to twelve monthly payments plus the return of the original amount, and to referral earnings for up to five people above the depositor. The dashboard shows your active principal — what you currently owe back — and what you have already paid in returns and referral earnings. Watch those three figures together.

Returns are a commitment, not a forecast The schedule is what you contract to pay a member, regardless of how the underlying investments perform. The risk notice shown to members says exactly this, and it should stay on the site. Anyone selling these plans should be able to explain the difference.

A note on cash

Because every deposit is cash, your written record of each handover is the only evidence that it happened. Keep counter receipts, and insist that members describe the handover properly when they file it — the system requires a description for exactly this reason.
