Why the backend is the largest single line

Most of what this platform does is arithmetic that has to be right every time and has to be able to explain itself afterwards. A monthly return is not one percentage — it is a rate that depends on how much was deposited and how long it has been held, fixed at the moment of purchase so that editing a plan never changes what an existing investor was promised. Referral commission walks five levels up the network on two separate events, and records the ones it deliberately skipped along with the reason. Every balance change writes one row carrying the resulting balance, so a statement can be reconciled without replaying history.

None of that is visible on a screen, and all of it is what makes the screens trustworthy.

All three services rise with usage WhatsApp, email and SMS are charged per message — at ten times the members, expect roughly ten times those three lines. Server, database and storage hold steady until traffic grows enough to need a larger machine. Unlike the earlier proposal there is no one-time store fee here, so year two costs the same as year one at the same volume.
A leaner start is possible The specification above is sized for a platform in use. Until member numbers justify it, a single smaller server with local backups runs the same code for roughly a third of Part B — at the cost of the standby database and the automatic failover. It is a reasonable place to begin and a straightforward thing to grow out of.

5 · What is and is not included

Included in the quoted price

  • All source code, handed over and owned by you outright
  • Two rounds of revisions at each design stage
  • Deployment to your server
  • Training for your staff on the administrator panel
  • 60 days of free bug fixing after handover — anything that does not work as specified is corrected at no charge

Not included

  • The infrastructure and service costs in parts B and C, which are billed by the providers
  • Any mobile application, and the store accounts that would go with one
  • Content: written copy, photography, and any legal text such as terms, a privacy policy or a risk disclosure
  • Business registration, financial licences, or regulatory approvals
  • Payment gateway integration — the platform settles in cash by design, verified by an administrator
  • A licensed market data feed, should live instrument prices be wanted from a real provider
