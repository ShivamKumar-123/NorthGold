Why the backend is the largest single line Most of the value is not what you can see. Slab-matched plans, schedules that cannot be altered after a member invests, payments that can never run twice, funds held the moment a withdrawal is requested, and commission that walks five levels up a referral chain — all of it has to be right the first time, because errors here are errors in somebody's money.

App store review is the one date nobody controls Google typically reviews in a few days; Apple can take longer and is stricter with financial applications. Both may ask for changes — business registration, a published privacy policy, or proof of the right to offer investment products. The submission fee above covers responding to those requests, but the approval date itself is theirs, not ours.

6 · What is and is not included

Included in the quoted price

  • All source code, handed over and owned by you outright
  • Two rounds of revisions at each design stage
  • Deployment to your server and publication to both app stores
  • Training for your staff on the administrator panel
  • 60 days of free bug fixing after handover — anything that does not work as specified is corrected at no charge

Not included

  • The infrastructure and service costs in parts C and D, which are billed by the providers
  • Content: written copy, photography, and any legal text such as terms or a privacy policy
  • Business registration, financial licences, or regulatory approvals
  • Payment gateway integration — the platform settles in cash by design. Quoted separately if you add online payments later.
  • New features requested after the scope in this document is agreed
