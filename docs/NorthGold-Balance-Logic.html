The one sentence version

Money on NorthGold is only ever in one of four places, and it moves between them on four events: an administrator approving a deposit, an investment starting, a monthly return falling due, and an investment maturing. Everything in this document is those four movements, explained.

A note on the word "balance" Available balance is the only figure you can spend. The other three describe money that is yours but not yet spendable — either because it is working, or because somebody still has to approve it. That distinction is the whole document.

Pending deposits are not added to anything A pending deposit is a claim, not a credit. It sits in its own card precisely so it is never mistaken for money on the account. Until an administrator matches it against the handover you described, no other figure on the page changes by a cent.

2. Why "Available" shows $0.00 after a deposit

This is the single most common question, and it has a one-line answer: the deposit was automatically invested the moment it was approved. It did not stay in the wallet, because leaving it there would mean it earned nothing.

What happened, step by step

  1. You filed a deposit of $20,000 and described the handover. Nothing was credited — it appeared under Pending deposits.
  2. An administrator checked it against your message and reference, and approved it.
  3. $20,000 was credited to your Available balance, and a line was written to your statement.
  4. Because auto-invest is switched on, the same $20,000 was immediately placed into the plan matching its size. Available went back to $0.00 and Locked in investments became $20,000.00.
  5. Your return schedule started running from that moment. The first monthly return is due one month later.

Steps 3 and 4 happen together, in one movement, which is why you never see the money resting in Available. Both lines appear on your statement, so the trail is complete: a credit of $20,000, then an investment of $20,000.

Auto-invest is a setting, not a rule of nature It is on by default because it is what members want — money that sits in a wallet earns nothing. An administrator can turn it off under Settings → auto-invest on deposit. With it off, an approved deposit stays in Available and the member chooses a plan themselves from the Invest card on the wallet screen.

So when does Available stop being zero?

On the first monthly anniversary of the deposit. Each month's return is paid into Available balance, where it is immediately withdrawable. Twelve months later the principal follows it. Section 4 shows exactly what those figures are.

Two timing details worth knowing

Months are counted from your deposit, not from the calendar. If your investment started on the 14th, every return falls due on the 14th. A member who joined on the 31st is paid on the 28th in February — the date is pulled back to the last day of a shorter month rather than spilling into the next one.

Withdrawals are held at the moment you ask, not when they are approved. If they were only deducted on approval, the same balance could be promised to several requests at once. Asking for a withdrawal therefore reduces your Available balance straight away; if the request is turned down, every cent comes back.

The right-hand column is the point: the principal never shrinks while the plan runs. Each month's return is new money calculated on the full $20,000, paid into Available, and free to be withdrawn the same day. At the end of month 12 the principal itself returns, and the member has $25,000 available against the $20,000 they put in.

Every month's return can be withdrawn as it arrives Nothing forces a member to leave the monthly return on the platform. It is paid into Available balance, which means it can be withdrawn that day, invested into a second plan, or simply left to accumulate.
If you would rather it compounded An administrator can switch Settings → where returns are credited from wallet to principal. Returns are then added to the locked principal instead of the wallet, so the next month's percentage is calculated on a larger base. The trade-off is that nothing is withdrawable until maturity.

Rates are the ones configured under Admin → ROI plans at the time of writing. Each investment freezes its own schedule when it starts, so changing a plan later never alters an investment that is already running.

5. Investing and withdrawing

Both actions draw from the same figure — Available balance — and both are on the wallet screen, directly beneath it.

The Invest card

Moves Available balance into a plan. Enter an amount and the matching plan is selected for you from its size, with the full month-by-month schedule shown before you commit. The schedule is fixed at that moment and cannot be changed afterwards, by anyone. The first return is due one month later.

The Withdraw card

Moves Available balance out to you. The amount is held the moment you ask, and an administrator confirms the payout. If the request is turned down, the hold is released back to Available in full, with the reason attached.

When both cards are greyed out They are disabled whenever Available balance is $0.00, because there is nothing to act on — and the card says which of the two reasons applies: money still locked in investments, or no verified deposit yet. Locked principal cannot be invested again or withdrawn early; it is released at maturity.

Deposit sits apart, at the top of the page

Deposit is in the page header rather than beside these two because it is a different kind of action: it brings money in from outside and needs an administrator before it counts. Invest and Withdraw only move money that is already on the platform.

The statement is the record

Every movement described in this document writes one line to All transactions, carrying the balance that resulted from it. Adding the lines up always reproduces the balance shown on the card — that is checked automatically, and it means any figure on the wallet screen can be traced back to the events that produced it.

6. Questions members ask

  • I deposited $20,000 and my balance says $0.00. Where is my money? In "Locked in investments", earning. It was placed into a plan the moment the deposit was approved. See section 2.
  • My deposit is approved but the total looks wrong. Check whether some of it is still under "Pending deposits". Only approved deposits move onto the account; a second, unverified deposit sits in its own card until an administrator gets to it.
  • Can I take my principal out early? Not by default. Locked principal is returned when the term completes. Plans can be configured to allow early exit with a penalty, but that is off unless an administrator switches it on for that plan.
  • When exactly is my monthly return paid? On each monthly anniversary of that investment's own start date, not on a fixed day of the month. The platform checks hourly and pays whatever has fallen due, so a return is never missed or paid twice.
  • I asked for a withdrawal and my balance dropped before it was approved. That is deliberate. The amount is held at the moment of the request so the same balance cannot be promised twice. If the request is rejected, it returns in full.
  • Do referral earnings go into the same place? Yes — commission is paid into Available balance, withdrawable immediately, exactly like a monthly return.
  • Why is my figure a cent lower than my own calculation? Every payout is rounded down to the cent rather than to the nearest cent. Across twelve months and five referral levels, rounding up would pay out more than the published schedule promises.
Still not adding up? Open Wallet → All transactions. Every line carries the balance that resulted from it, so the movement that caused an unexpected figure is always visible there.
